About

Stannum is an independent app studio founded in October 2025 and based in South Korea. I’m Jeongseok Ko, its founder, and I lead product planning, design, development, and marketing.

I create apps that combine useful everyday tools with original illustrations and characters. Year Journey and MiniTime are already available on the App Store. Building and releasing them helps me learn from real users and refine the experiences I create.

My main focus is MewCanvas, a drawing app that keeps your finger from covering your artwork by letting a cat’s paw hold the brush. Its development is nearing completion.
